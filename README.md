# IUPRN — India’s Unexplained Phenomena Research Network

> **Jaanoge nahi, to manoge kaise.**

**IUPRN (India’s Unexplained Phenomena Research Network)** is an independent research initiative dedicated to the systematic documentation, investigation, and study of unexplained phenomena reported across India.

The purpose of IUPRN is not to prove or disprove extraordinary claims in advance. Instead, we aim to **document experiences, preserve evidence, investigate locations, examine possible explanations, and make our findings accessible for further study.**

---

## 🔭 Our Mission

India has thousands of stories involving unusual experiences, unexplained observations, historical locations, strange environmental events, and phenomena that people find difficult to explain.

Many of these accounts are either:

* passed down as stories,
* discussed only through social media,
* presented without supporting evidence,
* exaggerated for entertainment,
* or forgotten over time.

IUPRN aims to create a structured research record for such cases.

Our mission is to:

* 📍 Document reported unexplained experiences and locations
* 🔎 Investigate cases through systematic field research
* 📷 Preserve photographs, videos, recordings, and other available evidence
* 📝 Record witness accounts and relevant environmental information
* 🧪 Consider conventional explanations before extraordinary ones
* 🗂️ Build a structured database of investigated cases
* 🌐 Make research and documentation accessible to the public
* 🤝 Encourage collaboration between researchers, students, investigators, historians, scientists, and local communities

---

# 🧭 Our Approach

IUPRN follows a simple principle:

> **An unexplained observation is not automatically evidence of a paranormal explanation.**

When investigating a case, we attempt to separate:

**What was reported → What was observed → What can be verified → What can be explained → What remains unexplained**

This distinction is central to our work.

We are interested in the **investigation**, not simply the conclusion.

---

# 🔬 Case Investigation

Each case may be studied through several stages.

### 1. Case Selection

Cases may originate from:

* Public reports
* Witness accounts
* Historical records
* Local communities
* Researchers
* Investigators
* Publicly available documentation

Cases are evaluated before field investigation whenever possible.

---

### 2. Documentation

We attempt to establish the basic facts of a case:

* Location
* Date and time
* Description of the reported experience
* Witnesses
* Environmental conditions
* Historical background
* Available photographs and videos
* Previous reports
* Relevant documentation

---

### 3. Field Investigation

When practical and appropriate, investigators may visit the reported location.

Depending on the case, this can involve documenting:

* Environmental conditions
* Architecture and geography
* Light sources
* Sound sources
* Electromagnetic conditions
* Temperature and humidity
* Human activity
* Wildlife
* Natural phenomena
* Possible sources of misidentification

The exact methodology depends on the case.

---

### 4. Evidence Analysis

Collected material is examined critically.

We distinguish between:

**Verified evidence**

Information that can be independently supported or reproduced.

**Reported evidence**

Information provided by witnesses but not independently verified.

**Unresolved observations**

Observations for which a satisfactory explanation has not yet been established.

**Possible explanations**

Natural, environmental, technical, psychological, historical, or other explanations that may account for an observation.

---

# 🧠 Critical Thinking

IUPRN does not begin an investigation with the assumption that a phenomenon is paranormal.

We consider ordinary explanations first.

For example, an unusual photograph may potentially involve:

* Camera artifacts
* Reflections
* Lens effects
* Compression
* Lighting conditions
* Atmospheric effects
* Motion
* Wildlife
* Human activity

Likewise, unusual sounds or experiences may have environmental, architectural, technological, or psychological explanations.

If an ordinary explanation adequately accounts for an observation, that explanation should be considered before extraordinary interpretations.

# 📍 Why Location Matters

A major part of IUPRN's research philosophy is that an unexplained experience should be studied in its **actual environment** whenever possible.

The location can provide important context.

Factors such as:

* Geography
* Architecture
* Weather
* Lighting
* Acoustics
* Human activity
* Wildlife
* History
* Infrastructure
* Nearby roads or industries

can significantly affect how an experience is perceived and interpreted.

Therefore, whenever practical, IUPRN aims to document the **location itself**, not just the story surrounding it.

# 🛡️ Research Ethics

IUPRN aims to conduct investigations responsibly.

We do not intend to:

* Harass witnesses
* Trespass on private property
* Encourage dangerous exploration
* Damage historical locations
* Manufacture evidence
* Manipulate photographs or recordings to create false conclusions
* Present speculation as established fact
* Reveal sensitive personal information without appropriate permission

Witness accounts should be treated respectfully, regardless of whether their interpretation is ultimately supported by the evidence.

---

# 🔐 Privacy

Personal information collected during research should be handled responsibly.

Where appropriate, sensitive information may be anonymized or withheld from public reports.

Photographs, recordings, interviews, and other material should be used with appropriate permission and attribution.

---

# 🧪 What Does "Unexplained" Mean?

For IUPRN, **unexplained does not necessarily mean paranormal.**

The term simply describes an observation for which a satisfactory explanation has not yet been established within the available evidence.

A case can therefore change status as new information becomes available.

For example:

```text
Reported
   ↓
Documented
   ↓
Investigated
   ↓
Possible explanation identified
   ↓
Explained / Partially explained / Unresolved
```

This allows the database to evolve as research improves.

# 📖 Research Philosophy

IUPRN follows a simple principle:

> **Observe first. Document carefully. Investigate critically. Conclude only when the evidence allows it.**

We believe unexplained questions are valuable precisely because they encourage investigation.

The objective is not to force every mystery into a predetermined answer.

The objective is to **find out what we can actually know.**

# 📜 Disclaimer

IUPRN is a research and documentation initiative.

Conclusions are subject to the quality and availability of evidence.
